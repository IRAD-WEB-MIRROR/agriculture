Draft order on the temporary suspension of imports of table potatoes

Status: draft. Comment period 16.10.2026-30.10.2026, closed. Comments received: 61. The final decision will be announced through the Department of Public Information.

After a record harvest of about 210,000 tonnes of potatoes in the Chu and Talas valleys the Ministry proposes to suspend imports of table potatoes until 1 March 2027, so that growers can sell their crop while the cellars are full. Seed potatoes are exempt. The order would be reviewed if retail prices rose.

MINISTRY OF AGRICULTURE AND WATER RESOURCES
OF THE IRAD SPECIAL ECONOMIC ZONE

ORDER (DRAFT)

__.__.2026    Talas    No. ___

On the temporary suspension of imports of table potatoes

  1. Import licences for table potatoes (fresh or chilled, other than seed) shall not be issued, and licences already issued shall be suspended, from the date of entry into force of this order until 1 March 2027.
  2. Seed potatoes accompanied by a phytosanitary certificate are exempt.
  3. Consignments in transit on the date of entry into force may be cleared on presentation of the bill of lading.
  4. The order shall be reviewed in February 2027, or earlier if the average retail price in the Bazar-i Kalan, Talas, rises markedly over two consecutive weeks.
  5. The Customs Service and the Talas Customs Clearance Terminal shall apply this order at all crossings.

Minister            M. Nurmatov

How comments were accepted

  • In writing to the Department of Crop Production, Jibek Jolu Avenue 41, 4th floor, room 409, Mon-Fri 9:00-17:00;
  • by post with the mark «Potatoes: comments»;
  • by e-mail to crops@agriculture.id.gov or by fax +996-39-2-24-60-49;
  • through the online reception of the Office of the Governor (e-irad.org/appeals/).

Each comment had to give the name of the person or organisation, an address and the item of the draft concerned. Anonymous comments were not considered. Comments sent after 30 October are not considered.
